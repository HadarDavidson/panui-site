Privacy policy: Panui

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Closed trial version. We will update this policy before opening to the public. If this English version and the Hebrew version differ, the Hebrew version applies.

In short

  • You can use the map without an account. Handing over spots requires signing in with Apple or Google.
  • When you publish a vacancy, the spot's location is sent to the server. Other users see it rounded to about 100 m, and whoever claims the spot sees the exact location.
  • Only if you turn on "Vacancy near me" (off by default) does the server keep your last location, rounded to about 100 m, for one day. A usual parking area is kept only if you set one, and you can delete it at any time.
  • Your vehicle type, disabled badge, active parking and parking photos are stored on your device only.
  • We don't sell data. You can delete your account and its data in Settings.

We also keep an anonymous record of every spot that was freed up or taken (place and time, no user ID), to estimate in the future the chance of finding a spot on each street.

A spot near your home: a vacancy you publish near your home reveals the area where you park, and whoever claims it sees the exact place. This is part of the service and worth keeping in mind.

Third-party services

  • Server and database: Supabase. The servers are outside Israel, in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). If we move them, we will update this page.
  • Sign-in: Apple and Google. They give us an email address and name, according to what you approved on their sign-in screen.
  • Notifications: Expo, Apple and Google.
  • Maps: Apple Maps on iPhone and Google Maps on Android. Map providers may collect technical information under their own policies.
  • Navigation: tapping "Navigate" opens Waze, Google Maps or Apple Maps, and their use is subject to their terms.
  • App updates (Expo / EAS Update): basic technical information is sent, such as the app version and device type.
  • Ads: in the trial version only a sample ad is shown, and there is no ad network. Before connecting an ad network (for example AdMob) we will update this policy and ask for consent where required.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell data or pass it to advertisers.
  • We don't collect location when the app is closed.
  • We don't show your name or email to other users.

On what basis we use information

  • Consent. Information is collected to give you the service you asked for, and only with your consent: there is no legal duty to provide it. You can use the map without an account, leave "Vacancy near me" off, not set a usual area, and refuse location permission (the map then just won't center on you).
  • Sensitive information. Location data can reveal habits, such as where you live or work. That is why we collect the minimum, round locations shown to others, and delete on the schedule below.
  • Transfer abroad. The servers are in the European Union, where data protection rules are at least equivalent to Israel's.

Information security

  • Communication with the server is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Each user can read and change only their own information (row-level permissions in the database), and exact locations are not shown to other users.
  • The sign-in token is kept in the device's secure storage.

  • Access to the server is limited to the Panui team.

  • Deleting your account: Settings → Delete account. This deletes the account, profile, points, vacancies and history. The anonymous records remain.

  • Data on your device: deleting the app deletes it. Ending a parking deletes the car location, the photo and the reminders.
  • Retention periods (automatic deletion once a day):
    • Your last location for "vacancy near me" alerts: deleted after a day.
    • The exact handoff locations (when claiming and when pressing "Handoff"): deleted 30 days after the handoff ended. The spot's own location is then rounded to about 100 m.
    • The notification log: deleted after 30 days.
    • Vacancies and handoffs: deleted 12 months after the leaving time.
    • Reports about users and signs of unusual use: deleted after 12 months.
    • Points and tier: kept as long as the account exists.
  • Your rights: you can contact us to see the information kept about you, correct it or delete it (sections 13 and 14 of the Privacy Protection Law). We will answer promptly and within the time the law sets. You can also contact the Privacy Protection Authority.

Changes to this policy

If we start collecting more information, we will update the policy before the change and ask for consent where required.
