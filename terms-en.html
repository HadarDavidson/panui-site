The map and the information

  1. Information only. Panui shows information about parking arrangements to help you decide where to park. It is not legal advice and does not replace reading the signs and markings on the street.
  2. The signs on the street prevail. Every sign, curb marking or instruction from a parking inspector or police officer prevails over the information in the app. Parking legally is the driver's responsibility.
  3. Trial version and simulated data. In the current version some street-level parking arrangements are simulated for demonstration. The open data of the Tel Aviv-Yafo Municipality may also be out of date.
  4. Rates. Rates and paid hours are shown according to the published municipal policy and may change. Parking itself is paid in the official parking apps, not in Panui.

Handing over spots

  1. Panui only passes on information. It lets people know that a driver is about to leave a spot on a public street. The spot does not belong to the driver leaving, to Panui or to the receiver, and it is not sold. There is no guarantee that the spot will be free, legal or suitable for your vehicle.
  2. No payment between users. Asking for or giving money or any benefit for a spot is not allowed. Anyone who does so may lose their account.
  3. Don't block the road or "save" a spot against the law. The driver leaving does not have to stay after the time they published, and you may not stop in a lane or double-park while waiting.
  4. Safety. Don't use the app while driving. Stop somewhere safe before doing anything, including claiming, navigating and pressing "Handoff".
  5. Meeting other users. A handoff is a meeting between strangers on the street. Panui does not check users' identities and is not responsible for their behaviour. Be respectful, and don't share personal details beyond what is needed.
  6. Prohibited behaviour. Publishing a vacancy that does not exist, claiming without meaning to come, using several accounts or another device to earn points, and false reports. In such cases Panui may hold points, limit or close the account.

Points, tiers and Premium

  1. Points are not money. They have no monetary value, cannot be redeemed or transferred, and are used only to decide the order in which vacancies are seen. Panui may change the rules for points and tiers and will announce a significant change in advance.
  2. Held points. Points earned from a handoff unlock after 24 hours. A handoff with unusual signals is held for review.
  3. Premium. In the trial version Premium is free and for testing only. Once it costs money, buying and cancelling will be done through the App Store or Google Play, and the price, billing period and renewal will be shown before you buy.
  4. Ads. You can watch an ad to move up a tier for a limited time. In the trial version a sample ad is shown.
